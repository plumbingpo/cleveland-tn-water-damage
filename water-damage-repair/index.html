Why Hire a Professional for Water Damage Repair?

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. Our team works from the moisture records taken during drying, so we know which studs were wet, which subfloor was treated and where to look twice. When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Cleveland

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Completed-repair photos and the scope document belong in your home records.
